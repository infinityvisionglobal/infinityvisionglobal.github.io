Delete your Lemar account and cloud data

Last updated: November 2026

Back to LemarDeutsch

This page applies to the voluntary Lemar account of the app Lemar (Infinity Vision Agency GbR). Without an account nothing is stored with us: your learning progress and settings stay on your device and disappear when you delete the app together with its data.

In the app

Open Settings in Lemar, then “Konto & Cloud” (account & cloud) and tap “Konto löschen” (delete account). After you confirm, your Lemar account, all cloud profiles and all cloud backups are deleted. You can optionally delete the learning progress on your device as well. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you no longer have the app or cannot access your account, write to infinityvision.global@gmail.com (including the email address or sign-in method of your account). We will arrange the deletion.

Subscription and other data

Deleting your account does not end a subscription. A Pro subscription keeps running and keeps being billed until you cancel it yourself. You have to cancel it yourself; we cannot do it for you because the subscription is held by Apple or Google. It is best to cancel before you delete your account:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings, tap your name, then “Subscriptions” and “Lemar”.
  • Android: Google Play, tap your profile picture, then “Payments & subscriptions”, “Subscriptions” and “Lemar”.

Deleting in the app removes your account and your cloud data. The record of your purchases at the subscription service provider we use is not deleted automatically. Write to infinityvision.global@gmail.com and we will have it deleted there as well. Payment and purchase receipts are held by Apple or Google and are subject to their retention periods and legal obligations.

If you redeemed a promo code, only a checksum, the time and the scope of the code remain after deletion, so that the code stays used up. Your email address is removed from this entry.

Which service providers are involved in account, cloud and subscription is listed in the Privacy Policy.
