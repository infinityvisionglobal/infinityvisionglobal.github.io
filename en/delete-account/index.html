Delete your Lemar account and cloud data

Last updated: November 2026

Back to LemarDeutsch

This page applies to the voluntary Lemar account of the app Lemar (Infinity Vision Agency GbR). Without an account nothing is stored with us: your learning progress and settings stay on your device and disappear when you delete the app together with its data.

In the app

Open Settings in Lemar, then “Konto & Cloud” (account & cloud) and tap “Konto löschen” (delete account). After you confirm, your Lemar account, all cloud profiles and all cloud backups are deleted. You can optionally delete the learning progress on your device as well. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you no longer have the app or cannot access your account, write to infinityvision.global@gmail.com (including the email address or sign-in method of your account). We will arrange the deletion.

What does not happen

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel a Pro subscription in the Apple App Store or Google Play. The customer record at the subscription service RevenueCat (identifier and purchase status) is not removed automatically by deleting in the app; write to us and we will have it deleted there as well. Purchase receipts at Apple and Google are subject to their own retention obligations. If you redeemed a promo code, only a checksum, the time and the scope of the code remain after deletion (so that the code stays used up); your email address is removed from this entry.
