2. Principle of data processing

Lemar is designed as a data-minimising learning app. Without an account, everything stays on your device: the app can be used entirely without a user account, and your learning progress, settings and personal app data are then stored only locally on the device you use. Only with a voluntary Lemar account are account data (email address or the identifier of the sign-in provider), profile names, learning progress and a random installation identifier for the device limit stored in the cloud (Google Firebase, database in Frankfurt am Main). Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google; RevenueCat processes purchase status and an identifier. This only happens once you sign in or open the subscription screen (see 4b). There are no public user profiles. Details are in sections 4a and 4b. Lemar uses no advertising, no advertising IDs and no user tracking for advertising or surveillance purposes. Anonymous usage statistics exist only if you expressly consent (optional, off by default; see 4d).

3. Locally stored app data

Depending on the functions used, Lemar may store the following information locally on the device: display name, avatar or avatar photo, age group, learning progress, coins, XP and level, achievements, favourites, quiz and learning statistics, settings, language and appearance, onboarding status, local notes and word lists, downloaded learning and audio files. Without a Lemar account, this data is not transmitted to Infinity Vision Agency GbR or to third parties; with a Lemar account, section 4a applies. Local processing serves to provide the app functions you use. Local app data can be deleted within the app or removed by uninstalling the app, except for files you saved outside the app, which this may not technically cover. The Family phrasebook is a private area on your device: the words and family expressions you write there are, like notes and favourites, included in the cloud backup only if you are signed in with a Lemar account. Your own recordings made there stay on the device only and are not part of any backup, export or the cloud. The offline learning pack only downloads audio files from the known audio hosts (see section 8) onto your device. Your choice of dialect and of learning profile are settings and are treated like other settings. Home widgets, Live Activities and Siri shortcuts show learning data (for example the word of the day or your daily goal) only on your device; for this, Lemar places it in a protected shared storage area of the app on the device. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties. Speech processing by Siri is carried out by Apple under its terms.

4. Save-state code

Lemar lets you create a save state locally as a code or file and restore it later. Such a save state may contain personal app data such as display name, avatar and learning progress. Lemar does not automatically upload the save state or transmit it to Infinity Vision Agency GbR. You decide whether and with whom you share a save state. Password protection can be used when sharing. Credentials, API keys, tokens and other secrets are not part of a Lemar save state.

4a. Lemar account and cloud backup (voluntary)

You can optionally create a Lemar account (sign-in with email and password, with Google or with Apple). Without an account nothing changes: everything stays on your device. Without an account, Lemar uses Google Firebase neither for sign-in nor for storage; the related software is merely made available technically (initialised) when the app starts. With an account, the following data is processed: account data (email address or the identifier of the sign-in provider, internal account identifier), profile names, avatar reference, level, and the learning progress and settings of the profiles. Photos and avatar images you upload yourself stay local on the device. The data is stored via Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore; provider: Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC), the cloud database is located in the Frankfurt am Main region (Germany). The cloud backup serves to restore your progress when you change device or reinstall, and to take it with you to another device. Device limit: so that a Lemar account is by default signed in on only one device at a time, we store in your account a randomly generated installation identifier of your app installation and the time of the last sign-in or activity. No device names, device models or hardware identifiers are stored; the identifier is not part of your backup file or of the cloud backup of your progress. The entry is removed when you sign out and deleted when you delete your account; if it could not be removed without a connection, it is replaced later or deleted with the account at the latest. A promo code can allow several devices to be signed in at the same time. The legal basis is the provision of the function you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For providers based or processing in third countries, transfer takes place on the basis of the applicable safeguards (e.g. EU standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision). Google may also process data in the USA; the safeguards mentioned apply to such transfers.

Besides your learning data, the cloud documents contain a technical identifier of the respective device and deletion markers (so that something deleted on one device is also deleted on the others). If you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo, if any, is loaded directly from Google's servers; as with any request, your IP address is transmitted to Google. Emails for account management (for example resetting a password) are sent by Firebase on behalf of Lemar.

You create an account yourself from the age of 16; younger users create it together with their parents or guardians (see 16a). The age group you can choose in the app only serves to adapt the content.

Deletion: In the app you can delete your account (Settings, "Account & cloud", "Delete account"); you can also request deletion by email to the address given under 16. Your account and the profiles and learning progress stored in the cloud are removed. Local data on your device remains until you delete the app or remove it within the app. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription; a subscription is cancelled in the Apple App Store or Google Play. The customer record at the subscription service RevenueCat (identifier and purchase status) is not automatically removed by deleting in the app; to have it deleted as well, write to the address given under 16 and we will arrange the deletion there. Purchase receipts at Apple or Google are subject to their own retention obligations.

4b. Pro subscription and purchases

Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we do not receive their payment data. To manage purchase status, Lemar uses the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). Without a Lemar account and without you opening the subscription screen, no data is transmitted to this subscription service (exception: you are signed in with a Lemar account; then the subscription status is checked at start). If you sign in or open the subscription screen, an identifier is created there; for technical reasons, IP address, app and device information and the store country are transmitted. In addition, RevenueCat processes purchase status, purchases and, with a signed-in account, the account identifier of your Lemar account so that Lemar Pro applies on all your devices. Processing may take place in the USA; the safeguards mentioned under 4a apply. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Without a purchase, no purchase data is processed. The purchase screen shows price, term and cancellation of a subscription from the respective store; cancellation takes place in the App Store or Google Play. Terms of use for the subscription: https://infinityvisionglobal.github.io/en/terms/ Promo codes: If you redeem a promo code, we store in our cloud database (Google Firebase) an irreversible checksum of the code, the time of redemption, your account identifier and the email address linked to your account (so that we can trace actions and help you with problems). The email address stays stored until you delete your account; when you delete your account it is removed from this entry, and only the checksum, time and scope of the code remain (so that the code stays used). The code is thereby bound to your Lemar account. Depending on the campaign, a code can be redeemed by several accounts (each only once); we then count on the code how many accounts have redeemed it and create a separate entry for each account. A campaign can also set a later start of the unlock; this point in time is then stored in the entry as well. Without an account and without redeeming a code, nothing is transmitted for this purpose. The legal basis is performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4c. Other backup options. You can optionally save a backup of your progress in your own storage: on Android in your Google Drive storage (for this you sign in to Google; the file is in your Google account, not with Lemar), on Apple devices in your own iCloud storage (the file is in your iCloud account, not with Lemar). The terms of the respective provider also apply to these storage services. The likewise optional encrypted rescue copy is only taken along by the device's operating system in its device backup; the password is kept in the device's protected storage.

4d. Anonymous usage statistics (optional). If you expressly allow it, Lemar records anonymous usage statistics to understand which functions are used and where the app needs to improve. This is switched off by default. You can switch it on at first start with the switch “Help improve Lemar”, you will be asked once more in a friendly way later at most, and you can change it at any time in the settings under “Privacy & Consents”. The consent is independent of the Terms of Use: Lemar works in full without it. Only events such as “lesson completed”, “section changed”, “quiz finished” or “purchase page opened” are recorded, with short identifiers (for example the lesson number), coarse group attributes (Pro or free, learning mode, interface language, chosen learning goal and level) and technical details that Google collects automatically (app version, operating system, device model, language, approximate country or region, usage time). A random app identifier is used, which is not linked to your Lemar account, your name or your email address. Not recorded are names, email addresses, learning content, entered texts, search terms, photos, recordings, your location and the advertising ID. The provider is Firebase Analytics by Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC; Google processes the data on our behalf and may also process it in the USA (see 14). Google uses your IP address only briefly for a coarse location estimate and does not store it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, section 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. When you withdraw, recording stops immediately, the app identifier is discarded, and events already recorded can no longer be attributed to any device. Event data is stored at Google for 14 months at most. Children and young people under 16 should give this consent only together with their parents or guardians.

5. Microphone and voice recordings

The microphone is used only when the user starts a pronunciation practice or voice input function. Microphone access only takes place after the user has granted permission. Temporary voice recordings for pronunciation functions are processed locally and deleted once processing is completed or cancelled. Where voice input uses a function of the operating system, speech recognition may, depending on the device and operating system settings, be processed locally or by the respective operating system provider.

6. Photos and files

Users can voluntarily choose their own image as an avatar and save content created by Lemar, such as certificates or save states, on their device. Access only takes place in connection with the function chosen by the user. Lemar does not search the device's photo or media library on its own.

7. Notifications

Lemar can show local reminders on request. For this, the operating system may ask for permission to show notifications. Lemar uses no push message server of its own for this and stores no push tokens on a Lemar server.

8. Online audio files

Some of the learning audio files can be loaded on demand from the external media provider ImageKit. When a file is requested, technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address and information about the requested file, is transmitted to the provider of the media server. Lemar does not transmit any personal learning progress, display name or user ID in doing so. The request serves to provide the audio function requested by the user; retrieved files are cached on the device. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in providing the learning audio files).

9. Currency converter

When the currency converter is used, Lemar may retrieve current exchange rate data from the external service open.er-api.com. No Lemar profile data or learning information is transmitted to the service. As with any internet request, the IP address of the device used is technically transmitted to the requested server. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in providing the exchange-rate function).

9a. Weather for banners

So that a sky banner matches the mood, Lemar retrieves the current weather from the service Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), for the fixed centre point of the Afghan province chosen in the settings. Neither the device location is used nor is a Lemar profile transmitted; for technical reasons the IP address is transmitted to the service. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in providing the banner function).

9b. Updates

At start, Lemar asks the respective store whether a newer version is available and, where applicable, asks you to update. On Android, Google Play's update function does this. On iOS, the app calls Apple's public version lookup (itunes.apple.com); for technical reasons your IP address, the app identifier and your device's country are transmitted, but no learning or account data. No server of ours is contacted. The update itself is delivered by the Apple App Store or Google Play according to their settings; the web version is updated together with the website in which it is embedded (see 9d).

9c. Downloadable languages

German and English are built in. If you choose another interface language, Lemar offers to download the corresponding language pack (it then stays on the device). For this, Lemar retrieves an index file and the language pack from a storage server (static file storage at Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Only technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address, and the indication of which pack is requested are transmitted; no Lemar profile or learning data and no account identifier. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in providing the chosen language). Without this function Lemar remains usable in German and English.

9d. Web version

The web version of Lemar runs in the browser and is delivered by the website in which it is embedded. When it is called up, the server of that website technically receives the IP address and other usual connection data; the operator of that website is responsible for this (see its legal notice and privacy policy). The web version stores learning progress and settings exclusively in your browser's local storage (not transmitted to us), insofar as this is necessary to provide the function. Lemar account, cloud backup and subscription purchase are currently not available in the web version. Sections 8, 9, 9a and 10 apply accordingly to audio, exchange rates, weather and the optional online services.

9e. List of festival dates

Lemar may occasionally retrieve a public list of dates (festivals and start dates of lunar months, each with its source) from a storage server (static file storage of an external provider, USA). A small file is retrieved for this; no learning or profile data and no account identifier is sent. For technical reasons, the IP address and the name of the requested file arise. The request is made at most every twelve hours, and over mobile data only if "Wi-Fi only" downloading is switched off. The most recently loaded list is cached on the device. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in showing festival dates up to date). Without this function Lemar calculates the dates itself and remains fully usable.

10. Optional external speech output

On first start, you can expressly allow Lemar to send text to online services using a separate, voluntary switch on the first page (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The switch is separate from accepting the terms of use; without it you can keep using Lemar without restriction. This covers: online translation (Google Translate, Google), speech output via the public Pashto community service (Pashto Ghag on Hugging Face, USA) and Microsoft Azure Speech where set up. Only the text to be read aloud or translated is transmitted, no account or learning data; the IP address arises for technical reasons. Without this consent Lemar remains usable offline with the dictionary and bundled audio. You can switch off online speech output in the settings; to withdraw your consent altogether, write to the address given under 16.

Lemar additionally offers an optional speech output, switched off by default, via an externally configured service, for example Microsoft Azure Speech or a server specified by the user. If the user sets up and uses this function, the text to be read aloud is transmitted to the chosen service. Credentials for these services are not part of a Lemar save state and are transmitted only encrypted (https://). Further processing is governed by the terms of the chosen provider.

11. Legal bases

Where personal data is processed, this takes place, depending on the function, in particular to provide the app functions expressly requested by the user under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where processing is based on consent, it takes place under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Such consent can be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future. Where processing is necessary to protect legitimate interests, it takes place under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. A legitimate interest lies in particular in the secure and stable operation of the app and in providing functions that technically require a request to an external server (sections 8, 9, 9a and 9c). You may object to processing on this basis on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). No personal data is used for personalised advertising or user tracking.

12. Storage on the device

Lemar stores locally the information needed for the selected app functions, settings and learning progress. Where storing or accessing information on the device is strictly necessary to provide a function expressly requested by the user, this takes place without separate consent within the legal requirements. Lemar does not use non-essential advertising or tracking technologies.

13. Storage period and deletion

Locally stored Lemar data generally remains on the device until it is removed by a delete or reset function or the app, including its app data, is deleted. Temporary voice recordings are deleted once their processing is completed or cancelled. Infinity Vision Agency GbR operates no user server of its own; account and cloud data of a voluntary Lemar account (including the list of signed-in devices) are held by Google (Firebase) and stored until the account is deleted; purchase data is held by RevenueCat (until deletion at your request, see 4a), Apple and Google according to their retention periods; the providers themselves determine the exact storage period. For external online services, technically necessary connection and request data may be processed as determined by the respective provider.

14. Transfer of data to third parties

Lemar does not transmit personal learning profiles for advertising or tracking purposes. Anonymous usage statistics are recorded only with your consent (see 4d). Data is only transmitted to external services where this is necessary for an online function used by the user. When a Lemar account is used, these are Google (Firebase) for sign-in and cloud backup; for a subscription additionally Apple or Google (payment processing) and RevenueCat (purchase status); with usage statistics switched on, Google (Firebase Analytics, see 4d); for the optional backup in your own Google Drive storage, Google (see 4c); for learning audio files, ImageKit (see 8); when the currency converter is used, open.er-api.com (see 9); when the weather banners are used, Open-Meteo (see 9a); when further languages are downloaded, the storage server (see 9c); and, only with your consent, the online services named in section 10 (Google Translate, Pashto Ghag/Hugging Face, Microsoft Azure Speech). Google, RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Hugging Face may process data in the USA. As with any internet request, technical connection data such as the IP address arises. This privacy policy names only the services actually used in the current release.

15. Security

Lemar uses HTTPS for external network connections, where these are part of the app. Credentials and API keys are not part of Lemar save states. We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, within the processing we control, against unauthorised access and loss.

16. Rights of data subjects

Where the legal requirements are met, there are in particular rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to certain processing. Access and portability: you can save your locally stored data yourself at any time as a file (Settings, "Backup & update", backup file; a machine-readable JSON file of your choosing) and take it with you; vocabulary can also be exported as a CSV/Anki file. With a Lemar account your progress is also stored in the cloud; you receive a copy of the data stored there on request by e-mail. Deletion: see 4a (in the app under Settings, "Account & cloud", "Delete account"). Consent that has been given can be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future; you withdraw consent to the online services (section 10) in the app under Settings, "Privacy & consents" by switching off "Allow online services". For questions on data protection or on exercising legal rights, the following address can be used: infinityvision.global@gmail.com. There is also the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; the authority responsible for us is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HmbBfDI).

16a. Children and young people

Lemar is intended for learners of all ages, including children. Children use the learning functions without an account; then everything stays on the device. Children and young people under 16 should use a Lemar account, the online services from section 10 and purchases only together with their parents or guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent; if we learn of such an account, we delete it. Parents can reach us at the address in section 16 and may request access and deletion at any time.

16b. Privacy rights for residents of California and other US states

Lemar does not sell personal data and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising; there is no advertising and no tracking in the app. If you live in California or in another US state with a privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia), you have, under the respective law and to the extent it applies to us, in particular the right to know what data we process about you and to receive a copy, to have data corrected and deleted, to opt out of sale or sharing (which do not take place) and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Processed – only with an account, a subscription or when online content is retrieved – are identifiers (e-mail address, account identifier, IP address), purchase status and your learning data. You can exercise these rights by e-mail to the address in section 16; an authorised agent may do so on your behalf; we verify your identity using the account and respond within the statutory time limits. We do not deliberately collect sensitive data within the meaning of these laws. Subscriptions renew automatically; the purchase screen shows price, term and cancellation, and you cancel in the App Store or Google Play (see 4b).

17. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy is adjusted when the data processing or the functions of Lemar change. The current version is always available within the app.
