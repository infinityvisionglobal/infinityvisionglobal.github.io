2. Local-first use and optional account

Lemar is designed as a data-minimising learning app. The app can be used without a Lemar account. Without an account, learning progress, settings and personal app data are generally stored locally on the device. An optional Lemar account can be used for cloud backup and to make learning progress available across devices. Lemar does not use personalised advertising or advertising identifiers.

3. Data stored locally

Depending on the features used, Lemar may store locally on the device information such as display name, avatar or avatar photo, age group, learning progress, coins, XP and level, achievements, favourites, quiz and learning statistics, settings, language and display preferences, onboarding status, local notes and word lists, and downloaded learning or audio files.

Without a Lemar account, these personal learning and profile data are not uploaded to Infinity Vision Agency GbR for cloud backup. Individual online features may nevertheless transmit technical connection data to the services described below.

4. Optional Lemar account and cloud backup

You may voluntarily create a Lemar account, for example using email and password or the Google or Apple sign-in options offered in the app. Lemar uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in and account management. Google states that Firebase Authentication is operated exclusively from data centres in the United States.

If you use cloud backup, the profile and learning data required for that function are stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Lemar's Firestore database is configured in europe-west3 (Frankfurt, Germany). Depending on the features used, stored data may include internal account and profile identifiers, profile names, learning progress, level, settings and technical metadata required for synchronisation. User-provided avatar photos remain local under the current implementation unless the app expressly identifies them as cloud content.

The Firebase services are provided by Google Ireland Limited and/or Google LLC. Where Google processes data outside the European Economic Area, the applicable legal transfer mechanisms and safeguards are used.

If you sign in with Google, the account information required for sign-in may be transmitted by Google to Lemar. If a Google profile image is loaded directly from Google within the app, Google technically receives connection data such as the device IP address during that request.

5. Account deletion

A Lemar account can be deleted using the account-deletion function provided in the app. The Firebase Authentication account and the associated Lemar cloud data are removed in accordance with the implemented deletion flow. Data stored locally on the device may remain until it is deleted within the app or the app data are removed.

Deleting a Lemar account does not cancel a subscription purchased through Apple or Google. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled through the relevant app store.

6. Premium subscriptions and RevenueCat

Premium subscriptions are purchased and billed through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Infinity Vision Agency GbR does not receive complete credit-card, bank-account or other complete payment credentials.

To verify, provide and restore Premium entitlements, Lemar uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat is not initialised merely because a brand-new user opens Lemar for the first time. A connection to RevenueCat is established in particular when you sign in to a Lemar account, open the subscription screen, start a purchase or use “Restore Purchases”. On later app launches, RevenueCat may also be contacted when a previously stored Premium or account-linking state requires an entitlement check.

When RevenueCat is initialised for the first time, an anonymous app user identifier may be created. RevenueCat may process this identifier, technically required connection and app/device information, the store country, and—where applicable—purchase, subscription-status and entitlement information. When you are signed in to a Lemar account, an internal account identifier may additionally be used so that the purchase status can be associated with that account. RevenueCat may process data in the United States.

This processing is used to perform and manage the Premium subscription and restore purchases. Current price, duration and cancellation information is shown on the relevant purchase screen and in the Terms of Use.

7. Additional backup methods

Where offered in the relevant app version, Lemar may also allow a backup in the user's own Google Drive storage or use iCloud/device-backup functions provided by the operating system. These methods are separate from the Lemar-account/Cloud-Firestore backup. The privacy terms of Google Drive, iCloud or the relevant platform provider also apply to their processing.

8. Microphone and voice recordings

The microphone is used only when you start a pronunciation or speech-input feature and grant the required permission. Temporary recordings used for local pronunciation features are deleted after the processing is completed or cancelled. Where speech input relies on an operating-system feature, recognition may be processed locally or by the relevant operating-system provider depending on the device and system settings.

9. Photos and files

You may voluntarily select an image as an avatar or save Lemar-generated content such as certificates or backup files on your device. Lemar accesses files or media only in connection with the feature you selected and does not independently scan the device photo or media library.

10. Notifications

Lemar may display local reminders if you choose to enable them. The operating system may request permission to display notifications. Lemar does not use its own push-notification server for this purpose unless a future app version expressly states otherwise.

11. Online audio files

Some learning audio files may be loaded on demand from the media provider ImageKit. The request transfers technically required connection data, in particular the IP address and information about the requested file, to the media server. Personal learning progress and Lemar profile names are not transmitted for this purpose.

12. Other optional online features

When the currency converter is used, Lemar may retrieve current exchange-rate data from open.er-api.com. For weather-based displays, Lemar may query Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) using a fixed reference point for the Afghan province selected by the user; the device location is not used for this purpose. These internet requests transmit technically required connection data such as the IP address, but not personal learning profiles.

If you enable an optional external text-to-speech service, the text to be spoken may be transmitted to the service you selected, for example Microsoft Azure Speech or a server configured by you. Further processing is governed by the selected provider.

13. Legal bases

Where personal data are required to provide an app, account, cloud or Premium function requested by you, processing is based in particular on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where processing is based on consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR applies and consent may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future. Where processing is necessary for legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR may apply, in particular for security and stable technical operation.

14. Retention and deletion

Data stored locally by Lemar generally remain on the device until removed through a deletion or reset function or until the app and its app data are deleted. Lemar-account cloud data are generally stored until the account is deleted or another deletion requirement applies. Purchase and subscription data held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are also subject to the respective legal and technical retention requirements of those providers.

If RevenueCat data have been associated with a Lemar account and cannot be fully removed through the normal account-deletion flow, you may request deletion at infinityvision.global@gmail.com; we will review and arrange the required deletion with the service provider where no retention obligation prevents it.

15. Recipients and international processing

Depending on the features used, recipients of technical or function-related data may include Google/Firebase, Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat, ImageKit, Open-Meteo, open.er-api.com and an external speech service activated by you. Firebase Authentication and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. Lemar's Cloud Firestore data are stored in europe-west3 (Frankfurt). Applicable legal transfer mechanisms and safeguards are used for international transfers.

Lemar does not disclose personal learning profiles for advertising purposes and does not use advertising identifiers.

16. Security

Lemar uses HTTPS for external network connections where supported by and applicable to the relevant service. Access to Lemar cloud data is technically restricted to authenticated users and their associated data. We use appropriate technical and organisational measures within the processing under our control to protect data against unauthorised access and loss.

17. Your rights

Where the legal requirements are met, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to certain processing. Consent may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future.

Privacy requests can be sent to infinityvision.global@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority.

18. Hosting of this website

This public website is provided through GitHub Pages. When the site is accessed, GitHub processes technically required connection data. This website itself does not include our own advertising or tracking technologies.

19. Changes

This Privacy Policy will be updated if Lemar's functions or data processing change. The current version is available through the app and this website.
