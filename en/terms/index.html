2. What Lemar offers

You can download and use Lemar free of charge. A large part of the content is permanently free, in particular the alphabet, the dictionary, streak and achievements, the first sections of the learning path, selected sections on Afghanistan and culture, and a sample of the other areas. Without a subscription, exercises are available within a small daily allowance.

A Pro subscription (section 5) unlocks additional areas, currently in particular the further learning path, exercises without a daily limit, adventures and the journey campaign, stories and audiobook, and several learning profiles. The areas currently included are shown in the app on the purchase screen before you buy; what is shown there at the time of your purchase is decisive.

Lemar largely works without an internet connection. Some functions need a connection, for example playing audio recordings that have not yet been saved, downloading further languages, the Lemar account, the cloud backup and purchasing a subscription.

Lemar is a learning offer. We strive for careful and correct content, but we cannot guarantee that you will reach a certain language level or pass an exam by using it. Information in the app about learning progress, plans and forecasts are estimates based on your learning activity and not a language test.

The content in the German section (for example about authorities, work, health or everyday life in Germany) serves language learning. It is not legal, tax, medical or other advice and does not replace such advice.

3. Use without an account

You do not need an account to use Lemar. Without an account, your learning progress and settings are stored only on your device. If you delete the app or its data or lose your device, this data may be lost unless you created a backup beforehand. The app offers you several backup options for this (see section 8).

4. Lemar account (voluntary)

You can voluntarily create a Lemar account, for example with your email address and a password or by signing in with Google or Apple. With an account you can use the free cloud backup and take your progress and a Pro subscription with you to another device by signing in there (see 5.6).

Please provide truthful information when registering and keep your access data secret. If you suspect that an unauthorised person is using your account, change your password and inform us.

One account should be used per person. Within an account you can, with Lemar Pro, create several learning profiles, for example for family members in the same household.

You can delete your account in the app at any time. This removes the account and the learning progress stored in the cloud; details are in the Privacy Policy. Deleting the account does not cancel a running subscription. Cancellation takes place in the respective store (section 5.4).
