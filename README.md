# Infinity Vision Agency / Lemar GitHub Pages

This bundle contains the official Infinity Vision Agency website and the legal/privacy pages for the Lemar app.

## Structure

- `/index.html` – bilingual Infinity Vision Agency home page (DE/EN)
- `/de/` – German Lemar legal hub
- `/de/datenschutz/` – German privacy policy
- `/de/impressum/` – German legal notice
- `/en/` – English Lemar legal hub
- `/en/privacy/` – English privacy policy
- `/en/legal-notice/` – English legal notice
- `/assets/style.css` – shared visual system
- `/assets/site.js` – XMB/PS3-style waves, particles, reveal effects, language switch and table of contents
- `/assets/infinity-vision-logo.png` – company logo

The Google Search Console verification tag remains present on the root page.
